Frequently asked questions

Anything else you'd like to know?

What does a custom website cost?

From € 1,500 excl. VAT. After our first conversation you get a fixed price before I start.

How long before my site goes live?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks. How fast it goes depends on the size of the site and how quickly the content is complete.

Do you work with WordPress or another CMS?

No. I build the site myself in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, without a theme, page builder or plugin layer.

Can I have things changed later on?

Yes. You can arrange that through maintenance with me, but you can also take the code with you and have changes made elsewhere.

Am I tied to your hosting or maintenance?

No. Once the site is delivered, the files are yours. Hosting and maintenance with me are a choice, not an obligation.

Is security included as standard?

Yes. I weigh security up while building and check the site for security and performance before it goes live.
